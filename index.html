About Àgídígbo 88.7FM

Agidigbo 88.7FM Ibadan is a leading broadcast radio station committed to truth, integrity, and responsible journalism. As the Radio of the Citizens, the station gives voice to the people and promotes transparency, justice, and development across society.

With a team of passionate broadcasters and journalists, Agidigbo 88.7FM continues to set the standard for quality radio in Ibadan and across Nigeria — earning the trust of millions of listeners every day.
